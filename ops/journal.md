# Journal

The record of the owner's rulings, corrections and Boss's decisions. **Append-only**: add lines at the end with `/log`; never edit, reorder or delete a line. A wrong line is fixed by a new CORRECTION line.

Shapes:
- `- <date> RULING — owner: "<exact words>" — means: <…> — reason: <…>`
- `- <date> CORRECTION — <date of the wrong line>: "<what it said>" was wrong; correct: <…> — evidence: <…>`
- `- <date> DECISION — <what Boss decided> — reason: <…>`
